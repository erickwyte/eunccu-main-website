Who Are We
Egerton University Njoro Campus Christian Union

Our God Reigns

"How beautiful upon the mountains are the feet of him who brings good news, who publishes peace, who brings good news of happiness…"

Isaiah 52 : 7

The Egerton University Njoro Campus Christian Union (EUNCCU) is a union of Christian students bound together in common faith in the one and only true God, committed to living according to the Holy Biblical Scriptures — exclusively non-political and non-denominational.
