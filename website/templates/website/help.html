EUNCCU E-LIBRARY

The eLibrary is a module under the main EUNCCU Website. Here is how you'll use the EUNCCU Online Library/ eLibrary.

1. Home Page (Under the Pages tab, choose E-Library)

The Home page has the following:

  • Recently Added Books (Count).
  • Book Search Bar.
  • Add Book.
  • Categories.

The Search Bar makes your work easier to get access to a book of your interest by providing a book title, author, or book number (Format:eg EUNCCU-001).


The Recently Added Books section displays the books that were recently added, by Title, Author and Category/Genre.


The Category section and page displays all the categories available. Makes it easy to find books of a certain section only by selecting a respective Category


The Add Book has a form to provide the book details and the pdf related to the information provide.
DISCLAIMER: Only upload a FREE Book, otherwise it won't be approved by the Developer, thus not be published.

2. View Books

In the 'Home Page', go to the book 'Details'. There you'll see a detailed infomation about the book

  • Read Online.
  • Download PDF.
  • Rate Book.
  • Add Favorite.
  • Book Review.

The Read Online button opens a pdf of the selected book to allow you read. Although some devices; mobile phones might not allow reading online but instead DOWNLOAD and open with your default pdf reader on your phone. But we're doing our best to ensure all devices serve the same purpose as expected

The Download pdf allows you to store a pdf on your device for later offline reading.

The Rate Book and Book Review allow you to rate a book in the range of 5 stars, and leave a comment which will be public. This depends with how you've interracted with the book. We request that you be genuine with the rating and commenting since it'll determine the book recommendation to others the more the book is rated, the more it'll be recommended to other readers.

The Add Favorites allows you to mark a book as your 'best' among them that you've interracted with.

3. Send Feedback

In the navigation bar, you can as well use the "Send Feedback" page to send us a quik feedback of how you've interracted with the system. Be it a recommendation, request, suggestion etc..

4. Other Pages

The Home page has the following:

  • Delete Request.
  • About.
  • Dashboard.
  • My Uploads.

The Delete Request. page has a form to allow you to send the request to the developer/leader for a book deletion from the system. You'll be required to provide a valid reason for book deletion since upon review, it's possible to also reject your request.
NOTE: You can only Request to delete a book that was Uploaded by you!


The About Us page gives a detailed information about the EUNCCU eLibrary; its purpose.


The Dashboard page has your record of Reading Progress, Books recommended for you, Books Read by you, Hours you spent reading books, your favorite categories


My Uploads Shows a list of books (with the upload details), you previously uploaded and were uproved.
