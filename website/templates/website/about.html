Who We Are

A Community
Rooted in Christ

The Eunccu is a vibrant, interdenominational fellowship of believers united by a shared love for Jesus Christ and a passion to see lives transformed by the Gospel. We are students, staff, and community members who walk together in faith, accountability, and joy.

Founded on the bedrock of Scripture, we exist to glorify God in every sphere of life — in our studies, our relationships, our service, and our witness to the world around us. We believe the local church and campus communities are powerful arenas for the Kingdom of God.

"We are not a religion. We are a relationship — with God, with each other, and with the world He calls us to love." — CU Founding Charter

Our Mission

Why We Exist

We are committed to cultivating a fellowship of Christian believers, equipped for eternal life and every good work, in the body of Christ and the society, through nurturing and training in God's word.


We do this by providing a space where faith is authentic, questions are welcome, relationships are deep, and service is a way of life. Our mission is not a programme — it is a life lived together in the name of Jesus.

Our Vision

Where We're Going

We envision to be Christian believers, holistically equipped for service and holy living.


We envision campuses and communities where the name of Jesus is honoured, where the lost are found, the broken are healed, and the hungry are fed — spirit, soul, and body.

Our History

Pillars Remembrance

FOUNDED

A Handful of Believers, One Big Vision

The Eunccu was founded by a small group of students who gathered in hostels like CBD, Maringo, Tatton, and Buruburu And lower field to pray and study the Word together. From that small beginning, something great was born.

YEAR 3

First Interdenominational Crusade

We hosted our first campus-wide evangelistic crusade, drawing hundreds of students and resulting in scores of decisions for Christ. This became an annual tradition.

YEAR 6

Formal Registration & Constitution

The CU was formally registered as an official campus organisation, establishing a constitution, governance structure, and accountability to the broader Christian community.

YEAR 9

Missions Department Launched

We launched a dedicated missions arm, sending our first teams to neighbouring communities and partnering with international mission networks to reach unreached peoples.

TODAY

A Movement Still Growing

With over 1500 + active members, 12 ministry departments, and a passion that burns as bright as ever, the eunccu continues to be a transformative force on campus and beyond.

Statement of Faith

What We Believe

The Bible

We believe the Bible, both Old and New Testaments — is the inspired, infallible, and authoritative Word of God, the supreme standard for Christian life and doctrine.

The Trinity

We believe in one God — eternally existing in three persons: Father, Son, and Holy Spirit — co-equal, co-eternal, and working in perfect unity for our salvation and the glory of His name.

Jesus Christ

We believe in the deity and humanity of Jesus Christ — His virgin birth, sinless life, atoning death, bodily resurrection, ascension, and personal return in glory and power.

Salvation by Grace

We believe salvation is by grace alone, through faith alone, in Christ alone. No human effort, merit, or religious observance can add to the finished work of Jesus at Calvary.

The Holy Spirit

We believe in the present work of the Holy Spirit who convicts, regenerates, indwells, sanctifies, and empowers believers for holy living and effective witness.

The Church & Eternity

We believe in the universal Church as the body of Christ, the bodily resurrection of the dead, and eternal life for the redeemed through Jesus Christ our Lord.
